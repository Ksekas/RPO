02

DESIGN PROCESS

/ FROM CONCEPT TO TRACK

Our development follows a four-stage iterative cycle. Each stage feeds back into the previous one — testing reveals issues that send us back to CAD, and CAD reveals limits that reshape the concept.

  1. 01
    PHASE.01 — IDEATION

    Concept

    Sketches, reference research, and aerodynamic theory. We study top teams, regulations, and physical constraints before drawing a single line. Every team member contributes initial ideas before we converge on a direction.

    • Regulation review
    • Reference benchmarking
    • Hand sketches & moodboards
  2. 02
    PHASE.02 — DIGITAL

    CAD Development

    3D modelling in CAD software with full dimensional control. Every surface is parametric, so changes propagate cleanly. We iterate on body shape, wing geometry, and weight distribution before committing to physical production.

    • Parametric 3D model
    • Mass & balance simulation
    • Tolerance verification
  3. 03
    PHASE.03 — PHYSICAL

    Manufacturing

    Translating the CAD model into a real object. CNC machining of the body block, careful sanding for surface finish, ballast placement, and final assembly. Tolerances are tight — a misaligned axle costs hundredths of a second.

    • CNC body machining
    • Surface finishing & paint
    • Wheel & axle assembly
  4. 04
    PHASE.04 — VALIDATION

    Testing & Iteration

    Track runs, timing, and analysis. Data from each run informs the next revision — sometimes a simple weight redistribution, sometimes a full body redesign. We treat every result as evidence, not opinion.

    • Run-time measurement
    • Defect & failure analysis
    • Revision planning
