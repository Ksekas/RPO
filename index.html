DESIGN PROCESS

Our development followed a three-stage process: concept, simulation, and manufacturing. ANSYS CFD analysis was used to evaluate aerodynamic performance, and simulation results directly drove two full CNC machining runs.

  1. 01
    PHASE.01 — IDEATION

    Concept

    Sketches, reference research, and aerodynamic theory. We studied top teams, competition regulations, and dimensional constraints. Every team member contributed ideas before we committed to a body direction.

  2. 02
    PHASE.02 — SIMULATION

    CAD & ANSYS Analysis

    We built a full parametric 3D model and ran CFD simulations in ANSYS to evaluate drag, lift, and airflow behaviour around the body. Simulation results informed geometry changes before any material was cut.

  3. 03
    PHASE.03 — MANUFACTURING

    Two CNC Runs

    The body was machined twice via CNC milling. The first run produced an initial physical form which we evaluated for fit, surface quality, and dimensional accuracy. Findings fed back into the CAD model, and a second run delivered the final bolide.
